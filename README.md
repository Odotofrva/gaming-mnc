# MENOCLONE GAMING

Open `index.html` to preview the website. Upload the folder to GitHub Pages to publish it.

The four images in `assets` are gallery placeholders. Replace them with screenshots when ready.

To connect your KICK livestream, set `KICK_CHANNEL` in `script.js` to your channel username. Until then, the site shows a friendly stream message.

Retro sounds are optional and off by default.


REALMS OF SE7EN: The 3D BMW E30 model is stored at assets/realms-e30-optimized.glb. The original upload was compressed by reducing embedded JPEG texture resolution and quality while retaining geometry. The original asset appears to be approximately 1.9 units long; the website scales it by 2.3 for an approximately 4.37 meter long life size AR presentation. Verify physical dimensions against your intended vehicle model before public release. Android AR uses supported WebXR/Scene Viewer; iOS Quick Look may require a USDZ asset for dependable AR support. The interactive 3D preview works independently of AR. The model-viewer component loads from a CDN and needs internet access.
