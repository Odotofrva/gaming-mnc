03 / OUR MISSION

ROOTED IN THE CLASSICS.
ALWAYS CREATING.

MENOCLONE GAMING is an independent studio with roots in New York City and Korea. We grew up inspired by the games and consoles that brought people together. Now we want to share those experiences with a younger generation and create something of our own. We develop hardware, software, and homebrew projects while staying curious about today’s games and the technology shaping tomorrow.
