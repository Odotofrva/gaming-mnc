01 // TRANSMISSION

PLAY THE UNIVERSE.
FIND WHAT OTHERS MISS.

REALMS OF SE7EN is being built around exploration, discovery and progression. Players travel across seven distinct worlds using the MENOCLONE craft, searching environments for digital products, hidden releases and rare unlocks that become available as progression grows.

The experience also connects the game world to real MENOCLONE products. When eligible physical products are purchased through the game, the item is shipped to the player in the real world — bringing the digital universe and the MENOCLONE lifestyle together in a new way.
